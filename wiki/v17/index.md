Also measured: a 417,792-byte heap with a 109,232,128-byte TOAST table cost **111,148,848 bytes of WAL** because every external value is fetched back and re-toasted; widening a referenced primary key moved `conpfeqop` to `{416}` = `=(int8,int4)` and drove **one seq scan over all 8,850 pages** of the 2,000,000-row child, which is never rewritten; row width grew by **0 bytes** on `(int,float8)` and `(int,int,text)` and by 8 on `(int,text)` and `(int,int)`, so "4 bytes per row" is never the answer; `wal_level = minimal` cut WAL to 161,016 bytes and `SET wal_skip_threshold = '4GB'` put it back to 26,315,416 (12.2: 151,928 either way, having no such GUC); and the add-column/batched-backfill/`CREATE UNIQUE INDEX CONCURRENTLY`/catalog-swap route cut the exclusive lock to a **305 ms** window but cost **1,634,508,136 bytes of WAL**, 3.8x the single `ALTER`, with the heap at 2.15x and `n_tup_hot_upd` 0 of 4,000,000. Logical replication into an already-widened copy works because columns map by name and text-format values go through the local type's input function - but `binary = true` broke apply with 6 `insufficient data left in message` errors on 17.11, an option 12.2 rejects outright. Twelve since-v12 changes are attributed to commits and first-release tags by presence tests at `REL_12_0`..`REL_17_0`, including `wal_skip_threshold` (v13), B-tree deduplication shrinking the rebuilt indexes from 179,855,360 to 118,243,328 bytes (v13), `reltuples = -1` (v14), extended statistics now dropped and recreated so their data is gone where 12.2 kept ndistinct (v14), bulk relation extension (v16, and the 34 extra blocks every 17.11 rewrite left behind), `pg_stat_io` (v16), and the read stream (v17). Unchanged: one WAL record per copied row, and no progress view, because `ALTER TABLE` never calls `pgstat_progress_start_command`.

Revised 2026-09-12 against eight reported defects, all confirmed in the pin: rule 3 now defines the **effective** per-table analyze threshold and scale factor (the reloption whenever it is `>= 0`, the cluster GUC only otherwise, plus the negative-`reltuples` clamp, the strict comparison and the `autovacuum_enabled` short circuit), which is what fixtures 94 and 95 override, and adds a two-point **statistics-publication** requirement because churn reaches `n_mod_since_analyze` only through a `pgstat_report_stat` flush while `pgstat_report_analyze` zeroes the counter and forgets changes committed during the `ANALYZE`; the oracle section records that a rebuilt or freshly built **empty** index keeps `reltuples = -1`, not `0`, so family 6 must keep an empty population apart from an unknown count; rule 2's drain no longer claims deletion from every index leaf (leaf order is key order, so the spread is fixture-dependent) and states volume-versus-distribution explicitly; test 120 must assert its intended zero estimate after the census because `ANALYZE` samples randomly; family 1 gains the **11b** `deduplicate_items` off-to-on transition control, which the old suite never covered; the `DEBUG1` verdict is narrowed (silent for `INCLUDE`, and only `_bt_leafbuild` asks for it) and the 2 % VACUUM bypass is given all four of its conditions; and the resolving-only citations are re-anchored to the actual replacement and build calls, with new caller/callee, generated-file (`genbki.pl`, `Gen_fmgrtab.pl`) and shipped-test subsections, the last naming the absence of any `make check` coverage for a launcher analyze decision or a bypass decision. The boundary is the skipped command, so fixtures whose point is a missing or stale statistics row (64, 83, 85, 97, 98, 108, 110 to 112, 118) stay. On 2026-09-12, tests 11, 11b and 38 were also retired: explicit deduplication settings are outside the suite, leaving 113 numbered tests with the remaining numbers preserved. On 2026-09-13 the page gained the **maintenance assumption** at the asker's direction: every fixture assumes normal autovacuum and auto-analyze maintain every table and stands in for them by hand, running `VACUUM ANALYZE` on every table its churn touched before the decide phase, whether or not the cluster or the table has autovacuum disabled; the launcher's vacuum and insert-vacuum thresholds are recorded from the pin but not applied, rule 3's census now decides only the tables no churn touched, and the cost is filed under Open Questions - 92 to 95 no longer isolate the analyze threshold, 118 coincides with 119, and the vacuum side is assumed rather than simulated. **2026-09-15: a fixture may no longer defeat the maintenance it issued.** A new `The maintenance must not be defeated` rule forbids five states from the first churn statement to the end of rule 3's census - an open transaction or held snapshot anywhere in the cluster other than a lazy `VACUUM`, a prepared transaction, a replication slot holding an `xmin`, a lock conflicting with `ShareUpdateExclusiveLock` across the maintenance statement, and a `statement_timeout`, `transaction_timeout` or `lock_timeout` short enough to fire inside it - because a `VACUUM` whose removal horizon is pinned collects no dead TID, never enters `lazy_vacuum`, and reproduces the dense-leaf shape of retired fixtures 65, 67, 113a and 113c while returning success. On B-tree it costs page recycling too: `_bt_pendingfsm_finalize` recomputes the horizon and stops at the first page `GlobalVisCheckRemovableFullXid` refuses, so `pgstatindex` keeps counting them as `deleted_pages`. Four proofs are recorded per fixture, a disabled launcher and the index-vacuum bypass are explicitly not defeats, no current test is exempt, and the proof's one-sidedness on the drain-exempt fixtures is the 15th open question.
- [Mandatory GIN Bloat Tests (unverified)](common-concepts/mandatory-gin-bloat-tests.md) - The wiki's shared **measurement protocol** for any claim about GIN wasted or reclaimable bytes in v17, extracted on 2026-09-15 from [Measuring Wasted and Reclaimable Bytes in a GIN Index With Contrib Extensions on PostgreSQL 17 (unverified)](questions/indexing/gin-index-wasted-space-contrib.md), which now links it instead of defining it. Protocol-scoped by design: it fixes the five phases per fixture (build, baseline, churn with a settle step, decide under the lock, and a measured `REINDEX INDEX` bracketed by `pg_relation_size(index, 'main')` as the only oracle), the settle step's proof obligations, the `SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE` measurement lock, the declare-then-score rule, the four mandatory cross-checks and the reading rules - and deliberately fixes **no fixture corpus**, which stays with the consumer page. Eight v17 behaviors are cited as the reason each rule exists: no single contrib function answers the question (`pgstat_relation` refuses GIN outright, `pgstatginindex` reads three metapage fields and no second page, and `contrib/amcheck` 1.4 has no GIN verifier); the entry tree never deletes a tuple, so dead keys survive every VACUUM and die only in a rebuild; a deleted page is reusable only once `GinPageIsRecyclable`'s horizon test passes, and `ginvacuumcleanup` re-reads the relation length rather than truncating, so waste is internal capacity and not returned bytes; free space means insertion capacity on an entry page (`PageGetFreeSpace` plus a line pointer) and a physical gap on a data leaf (`GinDataLeafPageGetFreeSpace` = `PageGetExactFreeSpace`); pending-list pages are deferred work whose flush drives keys through `ginEntryInsert` and can allocate through `GinNewBuffer`; the metapage's page counts are written by `ginvacuumcleanup` alone and an `analyze_only` call is a no-op outside an autovacuum worker; and `get_raw_page_internal` opens and closes the relation per call, so a multi-page census of a busy index is a sequence of instants. Also pins the three ways index cleanup silently does not run (`INDEX_CLEANUP` disabled, the wraparound failsafe, and the bypass that keeps cleanup), why `SHARE` is too weak (a plain `REINDEX INDEX` takes a self-compatible `ShareLock` on the table), the one hole no SQL lock closes (`gin_clean_pending_list` locks only the index and `LOCK TABLE` refuses an index), that the measurement lock's privilege is `MAINTAIN`/`UPDATE`/`DELETE`/`TRUNCATE` rather than `SELECT`, that a free index page reads back `MaxFSMRequestSize` rather than the `BLCKSZ - 1` the FSM was told, that the oracle is `maintenance_work_mem`-dependent and must be labelled, and that GIN has no `fillfactor` so density has no cross-opclass reference. **Extended on 2026-09-15 with the autovacuum, VACUUM, ANALYZE and auto-analyze rules ported from the sibling B-tree page, all binding**, which makes the churn phase end in a fixed order - the recipe's writes, the settle step, the maintenance step, then the census: a **maintenance assumption** in three parts (`VACUUM ANALYZE` on every table the churn touched before the decide phase; neither `autovacuum = off` nor `autovacuum_enabled = false` exempts a table; no fixture may leave churn unmaintained), the launcher's three threshold formulas recorded with their defaults, contexts and apply scopes but deliberately **not applied** on the vacuum side, and a **simulated auto-analyze census** that recomputes `relation_needs_vacanalyze`'s strictly-greater analyze test per table from the effective reloption-or-GUC values, with the negative-`reltuples` clamp, the `autovacuum_enabled` short circuit and two statistics-publication points. The port is not a copy, because a foreground `VACUUM ANALYZE` is **not** a faithful stand-in on GIN: `analyze_rel` enters ANALYZE-only index cleanup only outside a `VACUUM ANALYZE` and its comment names GIN as the only core AM that does not no-op there, so a worker's bare `ANALYZE` flushes the pending list and any other backend's returns at once. A fixture that models the auto-analyze-only state therefore runs `ANALYZE` **plus** `gin_clean_pending_list()`, and the page names the three ways that differs from the worker's path - `full_clean` true versus false, an index `RowExclusiveLock` with an ownership check versus the worker's table lock, and neither refreshing the metapage counts, so such a fixture is stale by construction, cannot claim the settle step and loses the metapage cross-check. Twelve open questions now, led by the absent corpus and including the six the port opened: the unsimulated vacuum side, the two stand-in differences that stand, the cross-check an analyze-only fixture loses, the unprovable second publication point, the `VACUUM`-written index count the mandatory `ANALYZE` now always overwrites, and the fact that no previously filed run was produced under either new rule. **2026-09-15: `The maintenance must not be defeated` added**, the same five forbidden states as the other two protocol pages, with a GIN-specific sting: with the horizon pinned `ginbulkdelete` never runs while `ginvacuumcleanup`'s `stats == NULL` branch still flushes the pending list, walks every block and rewrites the metapage counters, so **every settle-step proof passes with no dead entry removed**. The rule separates that from the expected one-`VACUUM` lag on freshly deleted pages, adds an entry-side proof obligation beside the settle proof, names three declared exceptions (the coverage-required held-snapshot fixture, the coverage-required index-cleanup-disabled fixture, and the measurement lock, which may not be held across either step), and files the one-sided proof as the 13th open question.
- [Mandatory Non-B-Tree, Non-GIN Bloat Tests (unverified)](common-concepts/mandatory-non-btree-non-gin-bloat-tests.md) - The wiki's shared **measurement protocol** for wasted-space and rebuild-payoff claims on every v17 index whose access method is neither B-tree nor GIN: hash, GiST, SP-GiST and BRIN are scored, and a four-part admission rule covers a non-core AM such as `contrib/bloom` or one installed by `CREATE ACCESS METHOD`. Protocol-scoped like the GIN page and **fixing no fixture corpus**: it fixes the five phases, the per-AM proof that the index work actually ran, the maintenance step and analyze census that stand in for autovacuum and auto-analyze, the BRIN summarization stand-in, the `SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE` measurement lock, one oracle (a measured `REINDEX INDEX` bracketed by `pg_relation_size(index, 'main')`) together with the per-AM inputs that oracle depends on, the declare-then-score rule, the per-AM instrument matrix, six cross-checks and the reading rules. **Every autovacuum, VACUUM, ANALYZE and auto-analyze assumption is carried over from [Mandatory B-Tree Bloat Tests (unverified)](common-concepts/mandatory-btree-bloat-tests.md)** - `VACUUM ANALYZE` after every churn, no exemption from `autovacuum = off` or a per-table `autovacuum_enabled = false`, no unmaintained churn, the launcher's three verdicts recorded with their five `PGC_SIGHUP` GUCs and six `ShareUpdateExclusiveLock` reloptions but applied only on the analyze side, and a census that recomputes `relation_needs_vacanalyze`'s strictly-greater test per table from the effective reloption-or-GUC values with two publication points and a `stats_fetch_consistency` read rule. Two findings make the port more than a copy. **On BRIN the mandatory maintenance grows the index**: `brinbulkdelete` removes nothing and `brinvacuumcleanup` summarizes every unsummarized range, so a BRIN fixture must be measured before *and* after the maintenance step, and an `autosummarize` fixture needs the stand-in (`brin_summarize_new_values()` or a targeted `brin_summarize_range()`) because the work item `brininsert` queues is fulfilled only by an autovacuum worker. **The auto-analyze stand-in, by contrast, is a plain `ANALYZE`**: all four AMs no-op in ANALYZE-only mode (hash by returning NULL stats rather than by testing the flag), which is exactly what `analyze_rel`'s comment means when it names GIN as the only core exception. Also documents why no single instrument spans the set - `pgstattuple` accepts hash and GiST and refuses SP-GiST and BRIN, `pgstathashindex` is the only AM-specific reader, `pageinspect` has decoders for hash/GiST/BRIN and **none for SP-GiST**, and `amcheck` 1.4 verifies nothing here - three different free-page registries (index FSM for GiST/SP-GiST, graduated heap-style FSM for BRIN, private bitmap pages for hash), the sparse splitpoint allocation that makes a hash file size a logical figure, a hash oracle that moves with the *heap's* `relpages`/`reltuples`, a GiST sorted build that ignores fillfactor, a deleted GiST leaf that `pgstattuple` counts as an empty one, and SP-GiST placeholders that only vanish in a trailing run. 198 distinct source citations, all resolving in bounds inside `raw/postgres-17/`; 13 open questions, led by the absent corpus, the four AMs' incomparability, BRIN's summarization-versus-bloat ambiguity, SP-GiST's unverifiable page classes, and the fact that no filed run has ever been produced under the protocol. **Revised 2026-09-15 with `The maintenance must not be defeated`**, filed after a review found the page enforced *issuing* the maintenance but never its effect: five forbidden states (a held snapshot or open transaction other than a lazy `VACUUM`, a prepared transaction, a slot `xmin`, a lock conflicting with `ShareUpdateExclusiveLock`, and a timeout that can fire inside the maintenance step), four proof obligations, and three declared exceptions - the coverage-required GiST held-snapshot fixture, the coverage-required index-cleanup-disabled fixture, and the measurement lock, which may not be held across a maintenance step. A pinned horizon turns a successful `VACUUM` into exactly the cleanup-only call the per-AM proof table already describes, so the protocol now fixes ten things, `## Why It Exists` names eleven properties, and the one-sided effectiveness proof is a 14th open question.
