Documents the option rejections (`PARALLEL`, `DISABLE_PAGE_SKIPPING`, `BUFFER_USAGE_LIMIT`), the absence of any cost-based throttling, the bulk-write path that hides the new heap's extends from `pg_stat_io` and defers its fsync to the checkpointer, and a since-v12 section attributing ten changes to their first release tags.
